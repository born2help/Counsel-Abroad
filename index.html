Why Partner With B2H

B2H Education provides a seamless platform for offices, counselors, and individuals to guide students abroad. Whether you have a local office or just want to support students, we offer **commissions, incentives, and full support**.

Universities can also connect directly to our verified student profiles for enrollment and admissions.

Opportunities for Partners

  • Earn commission for every successfully enrolled student
  • Access to global university programs and updates
  • Marketing & operational support from B2H
  • Flexible participation — no registration required

Opportunities for Universities

  • Direct student profile submissions
  • Priority verification and processing
  • Ethical partnership with transparent participation
  • Trusted global network for long-term enrollment

How It Works

1. Register

Sign up as a partner or university to access our platform.

2. Submit Profiles

Partners submit student details; universities share program info.

3. Approval

B2H verifies and approves submissions ethically and securely.

4. Earn & Connect

Partners receive commission; universities receive verified profiles.
